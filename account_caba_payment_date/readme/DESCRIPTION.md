This module controls the date of the tax cash basis (CABA) journal entry,
so that it always follows the real payment date.

Standard Odoo dates the cash basis entry on the date of the payment, but
when that date falls in a locked period it silently moves the entry to the
first open date. Besides, the tax lock date is only checked when the entry
is posted. This shifts the cash basis taxes (e.g. VAT effectively paid) to a
fiscal month different from the payment month without warning, which is not
acceptable in countries where those taxes are due on the payment date (e.g.
Mexico).

With this module the cash basis entry is always dated on the date of the
bank/cash entry of the reconciliation, and the tax lock date is taken into
account when choosing it. When that date falls in a locked period, the
behavior is configurable per company:

- **Block the reconciliation** (default): raise an error asking to reopen
  the period.
- **Use the first open date**: date the entry on the first day after the
  lock date.
- **Keep the standard behavior**: let Odoo choose the date (the first day
  after the lock date).

For vendor bills, the company can also choose to date the cash basis entry
on the latest of the payment and bill dates, for countries where the tax is
only deductible once the invoice has been issued (e.g. the Mexican
creditable VAT, which requires the CFDI).

When the date of a cash basis entry is shifted because its period is
closed, a traceability message is posted on the entry and its source
documents. The exchange rate difference entry generated by the
reconciliation follows the same lock criterion: when its period is closed,
it is dated on the operation date (today), also with a traceability message.
